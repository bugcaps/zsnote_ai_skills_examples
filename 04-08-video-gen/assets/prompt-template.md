## Video Prompt Structure

Runway, Sora 등 영상 생성 AI는 텍스트를 순차적으로 해석하므로, 일관된 구문(Syntax)으로 프롬프트를 작성해야 품질이 높아집니다. 다음 구조와 예시를 참고하여 조립하세요.

### 1. Structure
`[Camera Shot], [Subject & Action], [Environment/Setting], [Lighting & Color], [Camera Movement], [Style/Resolution]`

### 2. Element Examples
- **Camera Shot**: Wide shot(WS), Medium shot(MS), Extreme close-up(ECU), Drone view, POV
- **Subject & Action**: (최대한 구체적인 동작 묘사) A tabby cat walking gracefully, Astronaut floating slowly
- **Environment**: In a bustling cyberpunk street at night, On a rocky Mars surface
- **Lighting & Color**: Cinematic lighting, Golden hour, Volumetric rays, Neon glow, Moody atmosphere
- **Camera Movement**: Slow pan to right, Tracking shot, Push in, Handheld camera, Static shot
- **Style/Resolution**: 35mm film, Photorealistic, 8k resolution, Unreal Engine 5 render
